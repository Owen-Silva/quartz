Quartz isn’t your default browser

Windows asks you to confirm this yourself. Quartz opens the right page in Windows Settings for you.

  1. In the Windows Settings window that just opened, find Quartz at the top.
  2. Press Set default.
  3. Come back here. This page updates by itself when Windows says yes.

Step 4 of 4

What’s different here

Four things Quartz does that other browsers don’t, or not like this.



Workspaces

Separate sets of tabs for school, work or anything else. Switch from the pill in the title bar, or step through them with Ctrl+Alt+← and →.



Tabs that sleep

A tab you haven’t opened in 30 minutes sleeps and gives its memory back. Click it and it wakes where you left off. Change the timer in Settings › Tabs.



Home, your way

Every new tab opens Home: the time, your saved and most-visited sites, and live system stats. Press the pencil in its corner to move or hide any piece.



Find anything

Ctrl+K finds any tab, app or command. Ctrl+F finds text on the page, and Ctrl+H opens your history.
